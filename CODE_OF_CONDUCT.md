# Code of Conduct

I want this personal training repository to be welcoming to contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Discuss the code and ideas rather than attacking people. Respect different identities, accessibility needs and levels of experience. Accept feedback and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, insults, doxxing and publication of private information are not acceptable. Do not upload real student records or treat the fictional school scenario as a real institution.

## Scope and enforcement

This guidance applies to repository issues, pull requests and discussions. I may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and protect people who report concerns.

For abuse, use GitHub's Report abuse channel and avoid sharing private details in a public issue. For a software vulnerability, follow SECURITY.md. I cannot promise a fixed response time or confidentiality beyond the platform's controls.
